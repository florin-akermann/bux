# L0106 — brackets nest deeper than the parser descends

The parser descends once per level of nesting, and it stops at 32 levels.
Beyond that the limit is the machine's stack.
A program that overflows the stack cannot be reported on at all.

Canonical Lumen never comes near 32 levels.
An expression that does is asking to be given a name.
Bind a part of it with `:=`, or lift it into a function of its own.
